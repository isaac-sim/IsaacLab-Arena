# Robot-free motion scene gallery

The 30 workcells are standalone OpenUSD stages, in metres with Z up. Each contains collision geometry and two inspection cameras. The three controls reuse the existing pilot layout generators. The other 27 are inspection poses of the proposed short motions. The closing door, carriage and hood have USD animation at 24 fps. The cover shows one partly peeled configuration.

From the checkout's Arena runtime, export the source scenes and their RTX pictures with the following command.

```bash
isaaclab.sh -p isaaclab_arena_examples/spatial_memory/export_motion_scenes.py --output /tmp/g1-scenes
```

Use `--scenes dishwasher cabinet_reach` for a subset, `--seed 3847` to reproduce this gallery, or `--usd-only` to skip rendering. Open a generated `.usda` in a USD viewer and select `/World/Overview` or `/World/Approach`. The second camera crops out bench legs on tabletop scenes. For animated workcells the second picture is sampled halfway through the authored time range.

The camera poses, meshes and object names are for scene authoring and review. In a policy experiment the reconstruction would start empty and use onboard observations. These scenes illustrate physical constraints and proposed outcomes. Robot reach, contact mechanics, deformable peeling and policy success have not been validated.
