Measure the gear and pegs instead of estimating them from the images. Zoom with half_size about 40 around an object, then about 15 around the exact spot, then point. A peg top measures z = 0.100 to 0.115 m, the plate about 0.08 m, and the table 0.071 m; keep a peg point only in that range, otherwise zoom closer or use another camera. Measure at least two pegs and use their x spacing to tell which is the middle one. The base never moves, so keep using a confirmed middle-peg measurement. For the gear hole axis, point at two opposite spots on the flat hub top across the hole, never into the hole, and average them; measure the held gear again this way before and during step 5.
