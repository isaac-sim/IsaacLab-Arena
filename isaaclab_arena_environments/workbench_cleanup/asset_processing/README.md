# Workbench Asset Processing

The processed workbench assets use revision-pinned source USDs and add
wrapper-layer physics or visual overrides required by the cleanup tasks. Only
the workbench uses a structural recipe.


| Asset        | Wrapper changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Structural recipe changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Workbench    | • References the generated `structure.usda`. • Sets all four drawer lower limits to zero (prevents drawers from travelling behind their closed positions). • Makes the tabletop, hanging board, overhead shelf, four blue containers, and lower cross brace independent kinematic rigid bodies (keeps support and destination surfaces outside the cabinet articulation). • Enables contact reporting on those bodies (supports contact-based placement checks). • Uses small contact and rest offsets (reduces visible gaps and unstable contacts).                                                                                                              | • Selects the source Physics and PhysX variants. • Partitions the combined workbench mesh into the tabletop, cabinet frame, and named structural bodies (allows each task-relevant surface to have independent rigid-body ownership). • Creates one fixed-base `Cabinet` articulation from the cabinet frame, four drawer joints, and door joint (removes the original workbench-wide articulation without losing drawer or door motion). |
| Socket set   | • Keeps only the case base and lid (prevents loose tools and sockets from interfering with the lid task). • Creates one articulation with self-collisions disabled. • Removes the extra articulation root from the case base (avoids nested articulation roots). • Enables rigid-body contact reporting and disables sleeping on the case bodies (keeps object-control and task checks responsive). • Deactivates unused joints and the source physics scene (prevents unrelated controls and scene settings from entering the Arena stage). • Configures the lid's angular drive (provides stable open and closed lid behavior).                                 | None.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Oscilloscope | • Creates one articulation with self-collisions disabled. • Enables rigid-body contact reporting and disables sleeping on the body and controls (keeps button interaction responsive). • Adds collision settings and a small mass to the power button (avoids unstable mass inference for the movable button). • Configures a spring-return linear drive for the power button. • Converts every other control to a fixed joint (prevents unused buttons and knobs from moving). • Fixes the oscilloscope body to the world (prevents the device from moving when pressed). • Adds a black screen-off material (provides the target material for the power event). | None.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |


The source URLs and wrapper/recipe associations are defined in
`[assets.py](../assets.py)`. The wrapper layers live under
`[wrappers/](wrappers/)`, and the workbench recipe is
`[recipes/workbench.json](recipes/workbench.json)`.
